Tips that actually help
Read questions first

Read the questions before the passage. You're not reading — you're hunting. Skim for keywords, not meaning.

PEES paragraph logic

Point → Explain → Example → Summary. One tight paragraph per day builds more muscle memory than a full essay under pressure.

Predict before you hear

Use the 30-second gaps to read ahead and predict answers. Spelling counts — one wrong letter is a wrong answer.

Record everything

Your ear hears what your mouth misses. Record every session. Listen back. One session of this beats five unchecked runs.

The 4 PM anchor

Heavy cognitive work before your nap. Core habits after. Wind-down post-class. Structure is how you protect the streak without burning out.

Exam day

Arrive 30 min early. Bring ID. Don't review notes in the morning — trust your months of preparation. You've already done the work.
