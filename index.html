Tips that actually help
Reading first

Read the questions before the passage. You're not reading — you're hunting. Scan for keywords.

Task 2 formula

Hook → Position → Body 1 (claim + example) → Body 2 → Conclusion. Never skip the overview in Task 1.

Listening prep

Use the 30-second gaps to read ahead and predict answers. Spelling counts — check it.

Speaking recovery

Mistakes are fine. Correct yourself naturally and keep going. Examiners reward fluency, not perfection.

Streak mindset

On brutal school days, 20 minutes still counts. Showing up is the real skill you're training.

Exam day

Arrive 30 min early. Bring passport or ID. Don't review notes in the morning — trust your prep.
